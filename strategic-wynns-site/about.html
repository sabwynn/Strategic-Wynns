About Strategic Wynns

An independent practice in public finance, public administration, and good governance, based in Paonia, Colorado and serving clients across the country. Led by Stefen A.B. Wynn, M.P.A., ICMA-CM.

Led by Stefen Wynn

Stefen Wynn has spent more than a decade as the chief executive of small cities and towns in three states: Town Manager in Albion, Indiana; City Manager of Neptune Beach, Florida; and Town Administrator & Treasurer of Paonia, Colorado, leading organizations of up to 116 employees with operating and capital budgets of up to $18.6 million.

In Paonia, Colorado, where he served as Town Administrator and Treasurer from 2023 to 2026, Stefen inherited a water system stuck in place: a tap moratorium had sat on the books since January 2020, pending a completed capital improvement plan, verified water consumption data, and raw water metering. In the three years that followed, he finished those plans and assembled the funding, the first project was designed, funded, and delivered, and work that had been deferred for decades was finally moving. He left the town with its water projects fully funded and its finances in a much stronger, more secure place.

The funding record tells the story: a $9.7 million State Revolving Fund loan for the first phase of the water project, with $3 million in principal forgiveness, more than $11 million in grants and low-interest loans secured for water projects in all, two WaterSMART grants from the U.S. Bureau of Reclamation, and just under $2 million for the rebuild of a key intersection. Alongside the water work, Paonia adopted its first comprehensive plan since 1996, an effort that had been started three separate times before, earning a Vernon Deines Merit Award from the American Planning Association's Small Town and Rural Division. During his tenure the town also earned its first and second GFOA Distinguished Budget Presentation Awards, the first in its county. Throughout his career, Stefen has established monthly budget-to-actual reporting, codified financial and purchasing policies, developed five-year capital improvement plans, and brought delinquent audits current.

Strategic Wynns exists because organizations of every size deserve the same rigor, without the big-firm invoice. Stefen leads every engagement personally, and every deliverable is written so the people who pay for it can follow it. The same standard now serves municipalities, districts, utilities, nonprofits, and businesses alike.

“Leading with purpose. Building with heart.”

How we work

Independent

No software to sell and no construction contract waiting in the wings. Just analysis, and when the numbers say raise rates, that is what they say.

Transparent

The methodology is documented and open to anyone who asks, including any stakeholder at the microphone. Invoices show the work performed, hours billed, and what is still outstanding.

Plain-spoken

Reports are written for board members, staff, and the people affected, not for other consultants.

Respectful of small budgets

Scope is set for the organization you actually run, with a not-to-exceed authorization agreed before work begins, and advance notice before anything grows beyond it.

Bound by a code of ethics

Stefen remains an ICMA member and practices under the ICMA Code of Ethics: honest advice, disclosed conflicts, no gifts, and no misuse of the public trust that shaped his career.

Systems that outlast the engagement

Models, templates, policies, and schedules built to be understood and maintained by your own people long after the work ends.

Service area

Based in Paonia, Colorado, and working with clients across the country. Most work happens by phone, video, and shared folders, with presentations and work sessions in person when they matter most.
